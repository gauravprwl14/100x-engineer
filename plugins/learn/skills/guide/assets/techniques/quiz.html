Quiz

What it is: Multiple-choice questions that show the right answer and a short explanation after you pick.

What it does for the reader

  • Lets readers check understanding.
  • Explanations teach even when the answer is wrong.

Which redirect keeps analytics?

How to read it
  • Pick an answer; green is correct, red is your wrong pick; read the explanation.

How to build it: Buttons per option with the correct index stored. Markdown: options plus answer in <details>.
