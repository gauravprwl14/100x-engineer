KPI cards

What it is: A row of small cards, each with one big number and a short label.

What it does for the reader

  • Surfaces the 3–6 numbers that matter most.
  • Easy to remember and quote.
Best for3–6 headline numbers
Avoid whenNumbers that need comparison — use bars
FormatsAll formats

Example: The four headline numbers of the URL shortener.

~40/s writes (avg)
20,000/s reads (peak)
3 TB storage, 5 years
3.5T possible keys
How to read it
  • Big number first, label underneath explains what it measures.

How to build it: Grid of small boxes. Markdown: a 2-column table.
